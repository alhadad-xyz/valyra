# Valyra Hackathon Pitch Script
**Target Duration:** 2-3 Minutes
**Tone:** Professional, energetic, builder-focused.

---

### Slide 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:20)
"Hello everyone. We are Team Valyra, and we are building the exit strategy for the internet’s builders.

This is Valyra: an **Autonomous Marketplace for Micro-Startups**. We are bringing high-end M&A infrastructure to the micro-economy, built entirely on Base."

### Slide 2: The Problem (0:20 - 0:50)
"We identified a massive gap in the digital economy: the 'Micro-Exit'. 

Thousands of developers build profitable tools, plugins, and content sites worth $5,000 to $50,000. But selling them is a nightmare.
Professional brokers won't touch deals this small—their fees are too high.
So sellers are forced into Discords and Forums, where trust is zero. Buyers fear rug pulls, sellers fear code theft, and validating metrics is a manual, messy guessing game. 
This friction leaves millions of dollars in value 'stuck' and illiquid."

### Slide 3: The Solution (0:50 - 1:15)
"Valyra solves this by replacing the broker with **Code**.

We have built an Autonomous M&A platform. We use AI Agents to automate the trust, and Smart Contracts to automate the settlement.
This allows us to offer the security of an investment bank, with the speed and cost of a Shopify checkout."

### Slide 4: How It Works (1:15 - 1:45)
"Here is the core loop:
First, **Verification**. When a seller lists a project, they don't just fill out a form. Our AI Agent—powered by **Coinbase AgentKit**—connects to their GitHub and their Stripe Application. It cryptographically verifies that the revenue is real and the code exists.

Second, **The Settlement**. We use a Smart Escrow Vault. The buyer deposits stablecoins, which are locked on-chain.

Third, **The Handover**. Once existing assets are transferred, the contract atomically swaps the funds to the seller and the IP rights to the buyer. No bait-and-switch. No chargebacks."

### Slide 5: Why Base? (1:45 - 2:15)
"We chose **Base** because it is the only chain that makes this viable.
First, **Low Fees**. On L1, a $50 deposit would cost $10 in gas. On Base, it costs pennies.
Second, **Identity**. We use **OnchainKit** to integrate Basenames. Buyers and Sellers deal with human identities—like `alice.base.eth`—not scary hex addresses.
And critically, **AgentKit** allows our AI bots to actually interact with the blockchain, making them active participants in the transaction, not just chatbots."

### Slide 6: Market Focus (2:15 - 2:35)
"We are launching with a laser focus on **Indonesia**, one of the fastest-growing developer communities in the world.
That’s why we’ve integrated **IDRX**, a compliant Indonesian Rupiah stablecoin.
This removes the 'mental math' for our users. They pay in the currency they know, settled on the speed of Base, without ever worrying about FX slippage."

### Slide 7: Closing (2:35 - 3:00)
"Valyra is more than a marketplace. It’s a new liquidity layer for digital work. 
We are empowering the next million indie hackers to not just build, but to exit and reinvest. 
Thank you."
