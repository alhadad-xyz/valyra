## Section 2: The Product Demo (0:40 - 2:00)

### Part 3: Listing Flow (0:40 - 1:00)
**Visual:**
*   **User Action:** Click "Connect Wallet".
*   **Focus:** Show the **OnchainKit** modal connecting `seller.base.eth`.
*   **Action:** User clicks "Sell Asset" and pastes a URL.
*   **AI Overlay:** An Agent scans the URL, filling in "Tech Stack: Next.js" and suggesting "Fair Value: 45,000,000 IDRX".
*   **Action:** User confirms and signs with wallet.

**Narration:**
"Let’s see it live.
We start with Identity. I connect as seller using MetaMask.
Listing is autonomous. Our **Coinbase AgentKit** AI scans my SaaS, verifies the tech stack, and suggests a valuation based on real market data.
I sign the IP assignment on-chain, and I'm live in seconds."

### Part 4: Buying & Verification (1:00 - 1:30)
**Visual:**
*   **Switch to Buyer View (`buyer.base.eth`).**
*   **Action:** Browse Marketplace. Click on listing.
*   **Highlight:** Mouse hovers over "Code Verified" badge. Tooltip shows: "Build ID `abc...` matches GitHub Repo".
*   **Action:** Click "Buy Now". Modal shows "Depositing IDRX to Smart Vault".
*   **Animation:** Funds locking into a futuristic secure vault.

**Narration:**
"now from buyer pov they see the listing.
buyer spots the 'Code Verified' badge. Valyra cryptographically checks that the live site matches the GitHub repo—no bait-and-switch.
buyer pay in **IDRX**, our local stablecoin. the funds aren't sent to seller yet; they are locked in our Smart Escrow Vault."

### Part 5: Handover & Release (1:30 - 2:00)
**Visual:**
*   **Split Screen:**
    *   Left (Seller): Uploads "API Keys.zip". Animation shows successful **Client-Side Encryption**.
    *   Right (Buyer): Only *after* upload, button turns green "Decrypt & Verify".
*   **Action:** Buyer clicks "Confirm Receipt".
*   **Result:** Vault opens. 97.5% goes to Seller, 2.5% to platform.
*   **Final Screen:** "**sold!**" stamp.

**Narration:**
"The seller uploads the credentials.
Crucially, these are **encrypted** directly in the browser. We support Smart Wallets with a secure ephemeral key fallback, so Valyra never sees your secrets.
Sari decrypts the assets, verifies them, and clicks Confirm.
The contract instantly releases the funds. Deal closed."
